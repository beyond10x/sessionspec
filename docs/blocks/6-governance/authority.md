What may happen. A grant lets a principal or an agent revision perform an action class on a scope until it expires, as a standing permission, a standing approval requirement or a standing prohibition, with its conditions and exclusions; a budget caps tokens, money, wall time or tool calls of a principal, an agent revision, a run or a route, for its whole life or per call, day or month; a decision answers one request with a verdict, who decided, under which delegation and on which grant. Default deny: a request no grant covers is refused with a named error, never allowed by silence.
