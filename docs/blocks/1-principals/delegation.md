One principal acts for another. A delegation names who is acted for, who acts, the one decision the delegate may take (answer a question, approve, merge, publish) and when it expires. A chain of delegations must end at a human: the first link is granted by a human, every later link is granted by the delegate of a link that is still active. A delegation that would not end at a human, that closes a cycle, or that a delegate uses outside its scope is refused. An operator agent acting for its owner is the common case.

### Delegation

The delegate may take one kind of decision for the delegator, within the chain that ends at the root human. Active until revoked or expired; revoking a link ends the links granted from it.

### ActUnderDelegation
