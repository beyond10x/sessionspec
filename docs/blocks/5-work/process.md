The procedure a task follows, held by identifier only. A process has a name, a version and the digest of its source; it is published or withdrawn. Whether a step of a task is legitimate under the process is decided by an external evaluator, never here.

## Entities

### Process

One published version of a procedure. A task names it by ProcessId; the source itself lives with the evaluator and is known here by its digest.
