The one place model calls pass. A gateway holds upstreams, each an endpoint of `agentic.model` behind it. Every call by a caller principal on a route is decided once and recorded under its InferenceId: admitted, over-budget, rate-limited or no-upstream. An admitted call is completed with its usage and outcome; a refused call stays on record and cannot complete. A gateway that drains admits no new call.

## Commands

### AdmitCall

**Admit a call.** Decide one call and record it. The gateway names the upstream it picked for the route and reports what the caller's budget holds; the answers are admitted, over-budget, rate-limited and no-upstream, and each leaves a record. A call on an unknown gateway, or on one that does not serve, is an error and leaves no record. UNMAPPED: how the gateway picks an upstream for a route is not modelled.

Called by: Gateway.

| `gateway-not-serving` <span className="as-tag as-tag--refused">refused</span> | the Gateway of `gateway_id` has `state != Serving` | `GatewayNotServing`: The gateway is draining or stopped, so it admits no new call and takes no new upstream. |
