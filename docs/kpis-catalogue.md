142 KPIs over 23 blocks; 36 of them gates. A KPI has one of three kinds: **correctness** is a check that passes or fails, **completeness** is the share of what was promised that exists, **quality** is a number with a direction. A gate must be zero for a variant of an architecture to count. The status says where a KPI is computed: by `agenticspec kpi` on observed sessions today, by the simulator, by both, or not yet.

Filter by block, kind, status or gate, search by name, and sort by any column.
