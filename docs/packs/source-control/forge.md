A service that hosts repositories and the work around them: change requests, checks and the roles principals hold on it. A forge is registered, may be retired, and a retired forge hosts no new repository. A principal's role on a forge is a membership: granted by a principal, changed, revoked. A role says what the forge lets the principal do; what the system lets an agent do is still an authority grant (`agentic.authority`). Which product plays the forge is a binding, not this block.
