A change request: the ask to bring the revisions of a source reference into a target reference of one repository, what a forge calls a merge request or a pull request. It is opened at a head revision; every push moves the head. Checks and reviews are about one head: a check verdict or a review for an older head is refused, and an approval stays bound to the head it was given for. A merge names the head it saw and the authority grant it acts under, and is refused unless that is still the head, the head is approved, its checks passed and the grant is active. A change request may carry a proposal of a workspace (`agentic.workspace`) and deliver a task (`agentic.work`).
