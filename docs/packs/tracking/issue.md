A piece of work or a problem somebody wrote down: opened by a principal, assigned to one, labelled, closed with a resolution and reopened. An issue may become a task (`agentic.work`) and may be linked to the change requests (`agentic.scm.change`) that fix it or relate to it. The block holds no text, only the digests of the title and the body.
