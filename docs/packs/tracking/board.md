Work laid out in columns. A board has named columns in order; a card on it stands for one issue (`agentic.tracking.issue`) or one task (`agentic.work`) and sits in one column at a position. Cards move between columns and are removed; an archived board takes no new card and moves none. A board shows where work stands; who works on it is the assignment's concern.
