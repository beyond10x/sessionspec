Architecture

auto-merger

An agent that watches the change requests of one repository on a forge and merges each one whose checks passed at the head a reviewer approved, acting under a grant its owner issued. A recorded check result wakes it; it reads the change request, and merges or leaves it. The merge rule is the source-control pack's (agentic.scm.change.MergeChangeRequest): the named head is still the head, an approval binds exactly that head, its checks passed, and the grant is active. A push after an approval leaves the approval behind, so the merger waits for a new one. The parts of the pack (forge, repository, change requests, check runs) are named by their blocks; the simulator checks the structure and does not simulate them.
